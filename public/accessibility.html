Accessibility statement

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Policy version

We want everyone to be able to use AusWise, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control.

1. Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, consistent with the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth).

2. What we’ve done

  • Every page has a “Skip to main content” link and the same main menu, plus breadcrumbs on inner pages.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a clear focus outline. Dialogs keep focus inside them and return it when they close.
  • Text colours meet at least 4.5:1 contrast. Star ratings also show the number.
  • Form fields have labels, required fields are marked, and errors are announced.
  • Star ratings use a real radio group, so arrow keys and screen readers work.
  • Images have text alternatives. Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Animations respect your “reduce motion” setting.

3. Known limitations

Some links go to government websites that we don’t control. If you find something hard to use on our site, please tell us.

4. Feedback and alternative formats

Email . We aim to reply within 5 business days and can provide information in another format.

AusWise Migration provides general information and self-help tools. It is not a government agency and does not provide immigration assistance, legal advice or migration advice.

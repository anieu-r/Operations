Cookie Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Policy version

We set no cookies and use no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Our pages load nothing from other websites — even our fonts are hosted on our own server.

1. Browser storage we use

Some features keep small pieces of information in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device unless you submit a form.

NameTypePurposeKept until
auswise-storage-consentEssentialRemembers your storage choice so we don’t ask againYou clear it
agentTokenEssentialKeeps a registered agent logged in (only set if an agent logs in)Log out, or 7 days
auswise-sopOptionalSaves your SOP draft answers so you don’t lose themYou clear it or choose “Essential only”
auswise-apply-<visa>OptionalSaves questionnaire answers so you can continue laterYou submit, clear it, or choose “Essential only”

Optional storage is only used if you choose “Allow saving drafts”. Both choices are equally easy, and the site works either way.

2. Changing your choice

Use Storage settings in the footer of any page. Choosing “Essential only” immediately deletes saved drafts. You can also clear everything on the Your data page.

3. Payments

If you buy a paid extra, you are taken to Stripe’s secure checkout page on stripe.com. Stripe may set its own cookies there for security and fraud prevention, under Stripe’s privacy policy ↗.

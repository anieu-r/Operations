Your data & deletion

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Policy version

You are in control of your information. Most requests take a few seconds and need no email back-and-forth.

1. Delete an enquiry or questionnaire — instantly

Enter the reference number you received and the email you used. Both must match, so nobody else can delete your data.

2. Other privacy requests

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, deletion of anything not covered above, or make a privacy complaint. We respond within 30 days.

3. Agent accounts

Log in on the agent portal and choose Delete my account. Your profile, reviews and payout details are removed immediately.

4. Emails

Every email we send to applicants has a one-click unsubscribe link. Once you unsubscribe, we won’t email you again.

5. Data saved on this device

Remove any drafts and settings this site saved in your browser.

6. Not satisfied?

You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner ↗.

AusWise Migration provides general information and self-help tools. It is not a government agency and does not provide immigration assistance, legal advice or migration advice.

3. What we don’t collect or do

  • No cookies, analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, and no third-party scripts or fonts on our pages.
  • We don’t ask for your passport number in the questionnaire — you only need it when you lodge with Home Affairs.
  • We never sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • We don’t send marketing email. The only email you’ll get from us is a reference number you ask for, and replies to your messages.

4. Sensitive information

Health information and criminal record information are “sensitive information” under the Privacy Act. The questionnaire asks yes/no questions about these because they affect visa eligibility. Details are optional, and we collect them only with your express consent (a separate tick box), only to assess your visa options.

5. Children and young people

If the questionnaire is for someone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must give their name, email and consent before it can be submitted. Enquiry and contact forms ask you to confirm you are 18 or over, or a parent or guardian acting for a child. Agent accounts are for adults only. If you believe a child has given us information without this consent, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Who we share it with

  • Registered migration agents — only when you ask us to connect you with one.
  • Service providers who run the site for us under confidentiality obligations: our hosting provider, our email provider (to send you replies), and Stripe (payments).
  • The Department of Home Affairs — only for live VEVO checks run by authorised staff with the visa holder’s consent.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We publish agent profiles (name, MARN, bio, location, specialties, languages) and reviews. We never publish an agent’s email or bank details, or anyone’s questionnaire or enquiry.

7. Overseas disclosure

Our hosting, email and payment providers may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States, the European Union and Singapore. We use providers that protect information to a standard comparable with the APPs.

8. Security and retention

  • Connections are encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed with scrypt; login tokens are stored only as hashes; payout bank details are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Our site blocks third-party code with a strict Content Security Policy.
  • Enquiries, questionnaires and other submissions are deleted automatically after days, or sooner if you ask.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

9. Your rights

  • Delete — delete an enquiry or questionnaire yourself, instantly, with its reference number and your email on the Your data page. Agents can delete their account from their dashboard.
  • Access and correction — ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or for it to be corrected. We respond within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, by asking us to delete your data. Unsubscribe from any email with one click.
  • Anonymity — you can use the visa guides, finder and SOP builder without identifying yourself.
  • EU / UK users — you also have rights to data portability, restriction and objection. We rely on your consent and on providing the service you requested.

10. Complaints

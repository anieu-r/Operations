Refund Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Policy version

In short: everything on AusWise is free except two optional extras. Cancel before the work starts for a full refund. If the service isn’t delivered with due care and skill, your Australian Consumer Law rights apply.

1. What you can pay for

ServicePrice (total)
SOP review by a registered migration agentA$79
One-on-one session with a registered migration agentA$99

Prices include GST where applicable. There are no booking fees, card surcharges, subscriptions or automatic renewals.

2. Cancelling

  • SOP review: full refund if you cancel before the agent starts the review.
  • One-on-one session: full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before the session. Inside 24 hours, you can reschedule once for free.

3. If something goes wrong

Under the Australian Consumer Law, services must be provided with due care and skill, be fit for their purpose, and be delivered within a reasonable time.

  • If there is a major failure — for example the session doesn’t happen, or the review isn’t delivered — you can choose a full refund.
  • If there is a minor problem, we will fix it (for example by redoing the review or rescheduling) within a reasonable time, or refund you if we can’t.

Change-of-mind refunds after a service is delivered are not available, but this never limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

4. Government visa charges

Visa Application Charges are paid directly to the Department of Home Affairs, never to AusWise, so we cannot refund them. Home Affairs refunds them only in limited circumstances — see Home Affairs fees and charges ↗.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email with your payment receipt or reference. We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.

AusWise Migration provides general information and self-help tools. It is not a government agency and does not provide immigration assistance, legal advice or migration advice.
